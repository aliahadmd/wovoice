WoVoice

Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026. Cloud sync update.

What WoVoice processes

WoVoice sends a recording, approved glossary terms, and transcription options to Cloudflare Workers AI when you request a transcription. Audio, raw speech recognition output, surrounding editor text, and source-application identity are not retained.

Account information

WoVoice stores your verified email in encrypted form, token hashes, session metadata, account status, and usage required to provide and protect the free service.

Operational activity and administration

The sole WoVoice administrator can review verified account email, sign-in and session events, dictation duration and outcome, model, processing time, estimated AI usage, quota events, and the number and size of synced records. The administration console never shows dictated text, audio, dictionary contents, editor context, source applications, or synced record contents. Identifiable operational and administrative-audit events are retained for up to 90 days. Non-identifying daily service totals are retained for up to 13 months.

Administrator sign-in

The administration console uses a one-time email code, Turnstile, and short-lived first-party browser cookies. WoVoice stores only hashed administrator session and security tokens. It does not store passwords, one-time codes, IP addresses, or browser tokens in readable form.

Account restrictions and notices

Accounts that misuse or threaten the shared service may be suspended or banned. WoVoice records the action and sends a privacy-safe account notice. Questions and appeals may be sent to support@aliahad.com.

Cloud sync

When you sign in, your final dictation history, dictionary entries, and usage analytics are stored in your WoVoice account so every device you sign in on shows them. There is no recovery key: signing in is all a device needs.

Records are encrypted before they are written to storage, using a key unique to your account that is itself protected by a WoVoice server secret. Because WoVoice holds these keys, the WoVoice service can decrypt your synced records in order to deliver them to your devices. They are never shown in the administration console and are not used for any other purpose.

You control what is stored: turn off Sync history to delete your dictated text from your account and keep new dictations on the device that made them, or choose Auto-delete history to remove dictations older than 30 days, 90 days, or one year from your account and every signed-in device. Deleting an item or clearing history deletes it from your account too.

Earlier versions offered end-to-end encrypted sync with a recovery key. A device that still holds that key moves its records into cloud sync once, after which the old encrypted copies are deleted.

Deletion

You can delete synced items or your account from WoVoice settings, including while an account is restricted after fresh email verification. Logical account data and identifiable operational activity are deleted immediately; encrypted platform backups may remain for Cloudflare’s applicable recovery window.

Service providers

Cloudflare provides hosting, D1 storage, email verification and notices, Turnstile, and AI inference. WoVoice includes no advertising, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.
