WoVoice

Privacy Policy

Effective August 5, 2026. Administrator sign-in update.

What WoVoice processes

WoVoice sends a recording, approved glossary terms, and transcription options to Cloudflare Workers AI when you request a transcription. Audio, raw speech recognition output, surrounding editor text, and source-application identity are not retained.

Account information

WoVoice stores your verified email in encrypted form, token hashes, session metadata, account status, and usage required to provide and protect the free service.

Operational activity and administration

The sole WoVoice administrator can review verified account email, sign-in and session events, dictation duration and outcome, model, processing time, estimated AI usage, quota events, and encrypted-sync record counts. The administrator cannot see dictated text, audio, dictionary contents, recovery secrets, editor context, source applications, or encrypted synchronized content. Identifiable operational and administrative-audit events are retained for up to 90 days. Non-identifying daily service totals are retained for up to 13 months.

Administrator sign-in

The administration console uses a one-time email code, Turnstile, and short-lived first-party browser cookies. WoVoice stores only hashed administrator session and security tokens. It does not store passwords, one-time codes, IP addresses, or browser tokens in readable form.

Account restrictions and notices

Accounts that misuse or threaten the shared service may be suspended or banned. WoVoice records the action and sends a privacy-safe account notice. Questions and appeals may be sent to support@aliahad.com.

Encrypted sync

Final history, dictionary entries, and analytics can be synchronized as end-to-end encrypted records. The recovery key and plaintext are not sent to Cloudflare. Losing every device and the recovery key makes synchronized content unrecoverable.

Deletion

You can delete synchronized items or your account from WoVoice settings, including while an account is restricted after fresh email verification. Logical account data and identifiable operational activity are deleted immediately; encrypted platform backups may remain for Cloudflare’s applicable recovery window.

Service providers

Cloudflare provides hosting, D1 storage, email verification and notices, Turnstile, and AI inference. WoVoice includes no advertising, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.
