WoVoice

Privacy Policy

Effective August 5, 2026.

What WoVoice processes

WoVoice sends a recording, approved glossary terms, and transcription options to Cloudflare Workers AI when you request a transcription. Audio, raw speech recognition output, surrounding editor text, and source-application identity are not retained.

Account information

WoVoice stores your verified email in encrypted form, token hashes, session metadata, and usage required to provide and protect the free service.

Encrypted sync

Final history, dictionary entries, and analytics can be synchronized as end-to-end encrypted records. The recovery key and plaintext are not sent to Cloudflare. Losing every device and the recovery key makes synchronized content unrecoverable.

Deletion

You can delete synchronized items or your account from WoVoice settings. Logical account data is deleted immediately; encrypted platform backups may remain for Cloudflare’s applicable recovery window.

Service providers

Cloudflare provides hosting, D1 storage, email verification, Turnstile, and AI inference. WoVoice includes no advertising, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.
