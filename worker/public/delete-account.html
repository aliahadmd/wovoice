WoVoice

Delete your account

Account deletion is available inside the WoVoice Android app so the request can be tied to your signed-in device and a fresh email verification.

How to delete

  1. Open WoVoice.
  2. Open Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and complete the new email code.

What is removed

WoVoice deletes active sessions, synchronized encrypted content, and identifiable usage records. Local data assigned to that account is removed from the device. Audio and raw ASR text are never retained.

If you lost your device

Install WoVoice on another Android device, sign in with the same verified email, then use Account deletion. You do not need the recovery key to delete the account.
