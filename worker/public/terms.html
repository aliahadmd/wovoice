Administrative decisions are recorded in a limited audit log. Notices include only the account status, effective period, and public explanation. Internal administrative notes are not disclosed or emailed. Questions or appeals may be sent to support@aliahad.com.

Your data

You remain responsible for dictated and synchronized content. Do not dictate information you are not permitted to process. Synced history, dictionary, and analytics are stored encrypted in your account; the WoVoice service can decrypt them only to deliver them to your signed-in devices, and administrators are never shown their contents.

Your controls

You can turn off history sync, choose automatic deletion of older history, delete individual items, or delete your account at any time. Signing in on a new device restores synced content; no recovery key is needed.

Warranty
