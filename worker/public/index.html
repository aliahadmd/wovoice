How it works

Three steps. No typing.

  1. ⌥

    Hold or tap

    Hold ⌥ Option on your Mac, or tap the big microphone on your phone's WoVoice keyboard.

  2. Speak your thought

    Talk the way you normally would. No need to say “comma” or “full stop” — up to 60 seconds at a time.

  3. ✓

    Text appears

    Release or tap again. Punctuated, lightly cleaned text lands right where your cursor was.

WoVoice for Mac

Hold a key.
Speak anywhere.

WoVoice lives quietly in the background. Hold ⌥ Option in any app, talk, and let go — the text is typed at your cursor and your clipboard is left exactly as it was.

  • Quick-tap to dictate hands-free, or hold the middle mouse button
  • Choose your key: ⌥ Option, ⌘ Command, Caps Lock, or 🌐 fn

  • Only two permissions — it never reads your screen

Questions

Good to know.

Is WoVoice free?

Yes. WoVoice is a free public beta. Every account gets 10 minutes of dictation per day, which resets at midnight UTC (5:30 AM in India). There are no ads and nothing to buy.

Why isn't it on the Play Store or App Store?

During the beta, WoVoice is distributed directly. On Android, allow your browser to install the downloaded app when asked. On Mac, if macOS says it can't verify the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Which permissions does it need?

On Android: the microphone, and turning WoVoice on as a keyboard. On Mac: the microphone and Accessibility (Device Control & Data Access), which lets WoVoice notice your dictation key and paste the text. Nothing else.

Does it work offline?

Voice dictation needs an internet connection. The Android keyboard's regular typing mode always works, even offline.

Which languages are supported?

WoVoice is tuned for English (India) and handles Indian names and pronunciation well. More languages may come later.

How do I delete my data?

You can clear History or turn off History sync in the app at any time. To remove everything, delete your account — it takes a fresh email code and removes every synced item.
